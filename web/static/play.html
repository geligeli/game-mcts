How to play

Reinforce: click your territories to place armies (Shift +5, right-click −1, U undo). They are committed with your first attack or fortify.

Attack: click one of your territories with 2+ armies, then an adjacent enemy: every click is one roll with the most dice. Hold the mouse, Shift-click or turn on Blitz (B) to roll until it falls. Space repeats the last attack. A conquest moves in everything but one army with Move all on (A), and only as many as you rolled dice with it off.

Fortify: E ends your attacks; click a source and a destination of yours and choose how many move. Enter ends the turn without fortifying.

Camera: drag to pan, right-drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, C to reset. M mutes. After the round cap, territories decide, then armies.
